Since March 2017 I have logged every run, walk, ride, and hike I could measure. The result is a continuous paper trail of 8,252 km and 1,539 hours of movement — not a training plan, but a habit of paying attention.

The Movement

Walking carries the volume; running carries the ambition. Two platforms feed the record — COROS for GPS-accurate sport sessions and Keep for indoor training and habit check-ins.

  • Running: 509 sessions, 3,487 km, longest single run 29.1 km
  • Walking: 2,940 sessions, 4,015 km — the quiet backbone of the data
  • Riding & hiking: 24 rides (64.8 km longest), 20 hikes, plus 11 moto tracks

The Project

My Workouts is a self-hosted static site that turns raw activity files into a journal. Python sync scripts merge COROS .fit exports and Keep data into one data layer; the page renders it with no backend.

  • Annual heatmap, GPS track maps (Leaflet), and habit check-ins — all client-side
  • 303 habit check-ins logged: plank 94, situps 137, push-ups 69
  • Vanilla JS + HTML/CSS on GitHub Pages; inspired by zhaohongxuan/workouts
